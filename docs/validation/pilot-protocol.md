# First-time player pilot — pending

Recruit at least 10 consenting first-time players, with varied comfortable reach, hand size, lighting and camera positions. Record anonymous aggregate results; do not record camera video by default.

1. Explain local processing and allow a comfortable seated position. Do not coach the setup flow unless the player becomes stuck; record help given.
2. Time setup and first five returns. Target 8/10 complete within 2 minutes.
3. Counterbalance one-hand/two-hand order. Record catch rate, losses, control-history transitions and tracking interruptions.
4. Ask the cause of the most recent damage. Target 8/10 correctly distinguish drain, projectile or diver; inspect every tracking-related loss.
5. Observe voluntary retry without prompting (initial target 7/10).
6. Record before/after 0–10 effort and discomfort; investigate every report. Initial median effort increase target ≤2.
7. Run ordinary 50-crossing trials separately from deliberate occlusions. Require 0 identity swaps,0 uncommanded jumps >3% arena width,0 unintended mode changes,≤2 ambiguity pauses ≤500 ms.

Targets are design gates, not completed research or population claims. Compare the documented alternative drain rule only in a separate tuned build. If fairness interventions regularly exceed one per wave, revise attack patterns before adding density.

External motion/display filming needs explicit participant consent, hands/display-only framing where possible, local storage outside the repository, deletion after scoring and within 7 days, and no upload. An optical timing method can avoid retained video.
